The Journey Behind The Lens

I grew up surrounded by the rich cultural landscapes of West Bengal, India. From the vibrant terracotta temples and lush countryside to the teeming alleyways and misty hill stations, my artistic eye was shaped by contrasting textures, golden morning lights, and unspoken human bonds.

Over the past 3+ years, I have documented over 100+ photoshoots spanning raw portraits, destination travel diaries, fine art street scenes, and royal weddings. Every frame is crafted with patience, empathy, and respect for the natural environment.
